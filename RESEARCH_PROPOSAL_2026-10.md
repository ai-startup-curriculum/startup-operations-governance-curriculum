## Rationale

The 2026-10 live cycle sampled 45 in-window postings (2026-07-07 → 2026-10-07) across the equivalent-title buckets (COO / Founding COO / Fractional COO; Chief of Staff / Head of Business Operations; Head of People / VP People / CPO-people / Head of People Operations / Head of Total Rewards; General Counsel / Head of Legal / Fractional GC; Corporate Secretary / Assistant Corporate Secretary; Chief Compliance Officer / Fractional CCO; Data Protection Officer / Head of Privacy / Privacy Counsel). The sample intentionally widened the two buckets flagged as undersampled last cycle: Corporate Secretary moved from 1 in-window posting (2026-09) to 3 (2026-10) — Galaxy Digital Asst Corp Sec, Culture Amp Global GC & Company Secretary, Omidyar Network GC-as-Corp-Secretary — and Privacy / DPO moved from 0 direct in-window (2026-09) to 3 (2026-10) — Anthropic Privacy Counsel, Polymarket Director GRC & Privacy, Culture Amp (owning global privacy strategy). All 14 curriculum-owned themes continue to register posting-level support; theme-11 (Corporate Governance) rose from 0.32 to 0.33 and theme-12 (Enterprise Risk / Compliance) rose from 0.24 to 0.31 on the back of the better-balanced sample, both now at or above the 0.30 threshold; theme-14 (Operations Function Design) held at 0.53 (vs 0.56). The four emerging themes carried forward from the 2026-09 cycle were re-tested and all four remain below the { ≥ 3 in-window postings, ≥ 0.30 frequency, no existing module can extend } gate — AI-usage-policy / EU AI Act GPAI language appeared in 6/45 postings (0.13) and is already covered in mod-108 obj 6, mod-109 obj 6, mod-110 obj 9, mod-111 obj 4; fractional / interim executive delivery appeared in 3/45 postings (0.07) and is already covered in mod-104 obj 8, mod-109 obj 8, mod-114 obj 2; pay-transparency comp-band publication appeared in 25/45 postings (0.56) but is already covered in mod-106 obj 5 — the market has normalised the practice and it is now baseline posting hygiene rather than an emerging surface; RTO / in-office intensity as a first-class posting-level signal appeared in 32/45 postings (0.71) stating the RTO stance up-front, also already covered in mod-108 obj 5 and now baseline. Zero truly-new requirement patterns met the 3-posting threshold — specifically checked and ruled out: EU AI Act Article 55 GPAI named compliance (0 postings used Article 55 / GPAI language directly), SEC Climate Disclosure Rules (0 mentions), NY DFS Part 500 (0 direct mentions), EU DORA (0 mentions), UK FCA / HKMA (0 mentions in sample), Trust & Safety governance distinct from traditional risk / compliance (adjacent engineering-side T&S postings exist but did not reach legal-role-ownership scope at 3+ postings). Per the continuity-bias rule the delta this cycle is therefore empty — no new modules, no new exercises, no new projects. See `.aicg/job-requirements.json` `emerging_themes_below_threshold` for the audit trail of what was considered and rejected. Future cycles may promote the four emerging themes to exercise-level extensions inside the existing modules; that would be an incremental extension, not a new module, and would come through a separate delta proposal.
