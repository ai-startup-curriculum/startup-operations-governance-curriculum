# Exercise 05 — Termination playbook and severance / release drafting

> Estimated time: **~6 hours** · Related chapter: [05 — Voluntary and involuntary termination playbook](../05-termination-playbook.md)

## Problem statement

Cordwainer Media is a Delaware C-corp at Series-B, 110 employees, distributed across California (55), New York (25), Illinois (15), Colorado (10), and remote in Washington and Texas (5). You are the head of people. Three termination scenarios have landed on your desk in the same week and you need to execute each one — cleanly, defensibly, and with severance-and-release agreements that survive the state-law layer.

**Scenario A — Voluntary resignation.** Rowan, a Senior Product Manager, has resigned effective in two weeks. Manager received the resignation this morning. Rowan has been at Cordwainer 4 years, has been a strong performer, and is joining a competitor. Cordwainer has never run a proper exit-interview process; this is the first one you will design.

**Scenario B — Performance termination.** Simone, an L4 Software Engineer based in California, completed a 60-day PIP last week that concluded in release. She is 34, hired 3 years ago, on Cordwainer's standard offer letter and PIIA (per [mod-103](../../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/)). The manager, HRBP, and Director of Engineering are aligned on the release decision. Delivery is scheduled for Friday.

**Scenario C — Misconduct termination.** Damon, a Senior Marketing Manager based in New York, has been named in a harassment complaint filed by a peer three weeks ago. HR Acuity investigation, conducted by an outside investigator, concluded yesterday that the conduct occurred and constitutes a violation of the corporation's anti-harassment policy. Damon is 47. Cordwainer's CEO, general counsel, and outside employment counsel have concurred that the appropriate action is termination for cause, effective today.

Author the termination playbook — Scenario A's exit-interview instrument and voluntary offboarding runbook; Scenarios B and C's delivery-day scripts, severance-and-release agreements (state-specific for California / New York), and final-pay / COBRA runbooks.

## Requirements

### Part A — Voluntary offboarding runbook (Rowan)

Author the voluntary offboarding runbook for Rowan:

1. **Receiving the resignation.** Manager's response in the 1:1; the written confirmation from HRBP within 24 hours; the last-day confirmation.
2. **Exit-interview instrument.** The six-question instrument per chapter 05 (driver, what worked, what did not, one-change-as-CEO, rehire-yourself, anything to raise). Author each question with a rationale.
3. **Exit-interview logistics.** Who conducts it (not the direct manager per chapter 05); when; what data is captured; how confidentiality is preserved.
4. **Voluntary offboarding checklist.** Every line item from chapter 05 — last day, transition plan, knowledge-transfer, final-pay coordination, COBRA notice, equity communication per [mod-105](../../mod-105-equity-compensation-policy-and-comp-committee/), access revocation, property return, alumni-invitation.
5. **Feedback loop.** How Rowan's exit-interview data closes back into the performance-management operating system in [chapter 01](../01-performance-management-operating-system.md), the manager-training programme in [chapter 03](../03-development-growth-plans-and-l-and-d-programme.md), and the alumni / rehire-eligibility loop in [chapter 08](../08-alumni-rehire-eligibility-and-feedback-loop.md).

### Part B — Delivery-day script for Simone (California performance termination)

Author the delivery-day choreography for Simone:

1. **Preparation.** The day-before rehearsal; the single-page script; access-revocation timing coordination; paperwork prepared.
2. **The meeting.** Attendees (manager + HRBP; you as head of people join if HRBP is not yet in seat); location (video call since Simone is remote); length (under 15 minutes); the four-step structure (open with decision, one-sentence reason, hand to HRBP for logistics, close on next steps).
3. **The script.** The specific words the manager will say. Empathy + finality + clarity per chapter 05.
4. **Post-meeting logistics.** IT access revocation timing; team notification within the hour; HRBP follow-up 48–72 hours out.

### Part C — Severance-and-release agreement for Simone

Author the severance-and-release agreement for Simone. Address every element in chapter 05:

1. **Severance package.** Standard default policy — 2 weeks per year of service, minimum 4 weeks, maximum 16 weeks (or Cordwainer's specific policy — author it). For Simone at 3 years, land the specific package.
2. **Consideration.** The severance is the consideration for the release. Address the consideration-required-for-enforceability requirement.
3. **Release language.** All known and unknown claims arising from employment, with statutory carve-outs (EEOC charge right, state agency right, unemployment, workers' comp, vested retirement benefits).
4. **Consultation notice.** "The corporation advises you to consult with counsel of your choice before signing." Required by OWBPA for age-40+; sound practice for everyone.
5. **Consideration period.** Simone is 34 — OWBPA does not apply. Author the consideration window (chapter 05 references 5–7 business days for under-40). Address the California landscape specifically.
6. **Revocation period.** Author the revocation window; document that California does not require a 7-day revocation for under-40.
7. **Non-admission clause.**
8. **Return of property and continuing confidentiality obligations** under the [mod-103](../../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/) PIIA.
9. **Non-disparagement clause.** Narrowed per *McLaren Macomb* and Section 7 NLRA carve-out. Simone is a non-supervisory L4 engineer — the framework applies squarely.
10. **Speak Out Act carve-out.** Explicit carve-out for sexual-assault / sexual-harassment discussion.
11. **California Silenced No More Act (SB 331) carve-out.** Explicit carve-out for discussion of unlawful workplace conduct under FEHA.
12. **No non-compete.** California prohibits — see [mod-103 chapter 06](../../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/06-non-compete-landscape-and-alternatives.md).
13. **Equity treatment.** Post-termination exercise window per Cordwainer's plan (default 90 days for ISOs; longer if the plan permits at the cost of ISO status) per [mod-105](../../mod-105-equity-compensation-policy-and-comp-committee/). State the specific PTE deadline for Simone in writing.

### Part D — Delivery-day script and severance-and-release for Damon (New York misconduct termination)

Damon is being terminated for cause after a substantiated harassment finding. The mechanics are different from Simone's:

1. **Delivery-day script.** In-person if possible; general counsel or outside employment counsel on standby; immediate access revocation; brief-and-final.
2. **Whether severance is offered.** Chapter 05: severance is generally not offered for termination-for-cause; if offered (to secure a release), it is meaningfully smaller than standard performance severance. Author Cordwainer's policy decision — offer a reduced severance in exchange for a release, or terminate without severance and accept the residual claim risk.
3. **Severance-and-release agreement if severance is offered.** Author the release with the following material additions:
   - **OWBPA compliance** — Damon is 47. Author the 21-day consideration period, the 7-day revocation period, and the written consultation notice.
   - **Speak Out Act carve-out** — critical here, because the underlying dispute is sexual-harassment-adjacent. The pre-dispute non-disclosure / non-disparagement rules are void as against sexual-harassment matters; the post-dispute settlement is more nuanced. Address explicitly.
   - **EFAA / arbitration.** Address whether Cordwainer's standard arbitration agreement applies here — it does not, because the EFAA voids pre-dispute arbitration for sexual-harassment claims at the claimant's election.
   - **New York General Obligations Law § 5-336 and NY C.P.L.R. § 5003-b.** New York's Silenced No More statute imposes specific requirements on separation-agreement confidentiality clauses relating to discrimination, harassment, and retaliation claims — a 21-day consideration window (which may not be waived) and a 7-day revocation window. Address the interaction with the OWBPA windows.
   - **No non-disparagement covering the underlying misconduct.** The corporation cannot rely on a non-disparagement clause to silence Damon's factual account of the underlying conduct; the release should acknowledge this explicitly.
   - **The complainant's rights.** The separation agreement does not affect the complainant's rights; the release is between Cordwainer and Damon only.
4. **Reporting obligations.** If Damon holds any external professional licence (bar registration, etc.), state licensing boards may have reporting requirements Cordwainer should be aware of. Flag the question — do not author the licensing-law substance.

### Part E — Final-pay and COBRA runbook

Author the final-pay and COBRA runbook for both Simone and Damon:

1. **Simone (California).** Final wages, including accrued PTO payout, due on the day of termination per Cal. Labor Code § 201. Waiting-time penalty under § 203 up to 30 days if wilful. Execute via Cordwainer's HRIS (assume Rippling). Author the runbook — timing, calculation, PTO-payout mechanic, delivery method (direct deposit or check).
2. **Damon (New York).** Final wages due not later than the next regularly scheduled payday per NY Labor Law § 191. Author the runbook — timing, calculation, PTO-payout mechanic, delivery method.
3. **COBRA for both.** Employer notice to plan administrator within 30 days of qualifying event; plan administrator notice to qualified beneficiary within 14 days; 60-day election window; premium mechanics (100% of premium plus up to 2% administrative fee). Address whether Cordwainer will subsidise any portion of the COBRA premium as part of severance. Vendor execution — Cordwainer's benefits broker or PEO coordinates with COBRA administrator (WEX, Businessolver).
4. **State mini-COBRA layer.** Cal-COBRA for Simone (Cal. Health & Safety Code § 1366.20 et seq.; Cal. Ins. Code § 10128.50 et seq.) — potential to extend federal COBRA up to 36 months of total coverage. NY Ins. Law § 3221(m) for Damon — up to 36 months of continuation. Address.

### Part F — Termination checklist and post-mortem template

1. **The single-page termination checklist** from chapter 05 — populate every line item for Simone's and Damon's cases. Include decision documentation, employment counsel review, severance / release paperwork prep, delivery-day meeting, IT access revocation, team notification, HRBP follow-up, signed release, COBRA notice, alumni signalling.
2. **Post-mortem template.** For involuntary terminations, Cordwainer will run a lightweight post-mortem 30 days after each termination. Author the template — what worked in the delivery choreography; what the manager or HRBP would change; whether the severance package landed appropriately; whether any signal of legal exposure emerged. The post-mortem informs the manager-training programme in [chapter 03](../03-development-growth-plans-and-l-and-d-programme.md).

## Starter guidance

- Chapter 05 is the primary reference. The two branches (voluntary and involuntary), the three involuntary categories (misconduct, performance, role elimination), the delivery-day script, the severance-and-release agreement structure, and the final-pay / COBRA mechanics are the frame.
- Every severance-and-release agreement you author needs a state-law-specific carve-out (California SB 331 for Simone; New York § 5-336 and § 5003-b for Damon). Do not rely on the federal Speak Out Act alone.
- OWBPA applies to Damon (age 47) but not to Simone (age 34). The consideration and revocation periods differ accordingly. Get the OWBPA math right — the 21-day consideration and 7-day revocation are load-bearing.
- The *McLaren Macomb* NLRA constraint on non-disparagement applies to Simone (non-supervisory L4 engineer). Whether it applies to Damon (Senior Marketing Manager) depends on his supervisory status — the framework has historically been more permissive for supervisors. Address the question; do not assume.
- The Speak Out Act framework voids *pre-dispute* NDA / non-disparagement clauses relating to sexual-harassment and sexual-assault disputes. Post-dispute settlements after a specific dispute has arisen are more nuanced. Damon's fact pattern is the reason to include the carve-out explicitly and is also the reason the corporation cannot silence the underlying account through non-disparagement.
- The EFAA (9 U.S.C. §§ 401–402) voids pre-dispute arbitration for sexual-harassment / sexual-assault claims *at the claimant's election*. This is relevant to Damon's fact pattern (the complainant's rights are unaffected by Damon's separation); the exercise wants the recognition of the framework.
- The California final-pay timing (Cal. Labor Code § 201; same-day for involuntary) is not optional. § 203 waiting-time penalties are real and are asserted regularly.
- COBRA mechanics are executed by the benefits broker or PEO, not by the head of people directly. The runbook is about coordination — timing, calculation, and delivery via the vendor.
- Damon's misconduct case has legal-risk considerations that go beyond the release drafting. Outside employment counsel involvement is essentially required; document the counsel review.
- Do not invent statutory citations or state-law analyses beyond what chapter 05 and mod-103 support. Where a specific state-law application is uncertain, flag `<!-- needs-research -->` and cite what would need to be verified with local counsel.
- Equity treatment on separation defers to [mod-105](../../mod-105-equity-compensation-policy-and-comp-committee/). Name the specific handoff points (post-termination exercise window, vested-share summary, plan-administrator notification) without authoring the equity-policy substance.
