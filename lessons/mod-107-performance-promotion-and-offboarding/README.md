# mod-107 — Performance, Promotion, Development & Offboarding

> Feedback, PIPs, severance, and layoffs under WARN — the operating system that starts at the 90-day handoff from onboarding and ends the day an employee is either promoted, developed, coached out, or laid off.

**Track:** Startup Operations & Governance (level 50) · **Stage:** SEED / SERIES-A / SERIES-B / GROWTH · **Pillar:** people-ops · **Hours:** 32

## Why this module exists

[mod-104](../mod-104-hiring-onboarding-and-hr-operations/) handed the new hire off at the 90-day mark. [mod-105](../mod-105-equity-compensation-policy-and-comp-committee/) authored the equity policy and the compensation committee. [mod-106](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/) built the levels, bands, and the annual comp cycle that reads a *performance rating* out of the operating system this module owns. What none of those modules do is answer the two questions every people-ops function eventually has to answer, for every employee, every quarter:

1. **How is this person doing, and what changes as a result?** — the performance / promotion / development side.
2. **When is it time to end this, and how do we do that without breaking the person, the team, or the corporation?** — the PIP / termination / layoff / exec-offboarding side.

This module owns the operating system that answers both.

The performance side decomposes into three operating decisions:

1. **A performance-management operating system.** A review cadence (semi-annual formal review plus continuous informal feedback, calendared to feed the [annual comp cycle](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/04-annual-comp-cycle.md)); a review-format choice by level (self + manager + peer + upward + skip-level + 360 by level); calibration norms across functions and levels; a manager-training programme for writing and delivering the review; and a tool decision (Lattice / Culture Amp / 15Five / Betterworks vs. an in-house spreadsheet at seed).
2. **A promotion architecture.** A promotion-committee construction that reaches across functions; competency-anchored, sponsor-anchored, impact-anchored promotion criteria; a semi-annual cadence with an off-cycle exception path for exceptional cases; a promotion-communication playbook; and a not-promoted-signal-management playbook that catches the third of the pool who did not get promoted before they resign.
3. **A development / growth-plan / career-conversation programme.** A semi-annual growth conversation, an individual development plan (IDP) template, a manager-coaching programme (BetterUp / Better Manager / internal), an L&D budget at ~$1–3k per employee per year at Series-A (higher at exec level), an L&D-tool selection (LinkedIn Learning / Udemy Business / MasterClass / Coursera for Business / Reforge), and an internal-mobility programme that lets people move across teams without quitting.

The exit side decomposes into four:

4. **The PIP (Performance Improvement Plan).** Trigger criteria (documented performance gap after coaching), structure (30 / 60 / 90 day options with measurable goals and weekly check-ins), manager-support and HRBP-coaching patterns, the release-and-severance decision at conclusion, and the anti-abuse operating norm — PIP as coaching tool, not paper trail to fire.
5. **The voluntary and involuntary termination playbook.** Voluntary-resignation exit interview; involuntary decision framework (misconduct / performance / role elimination); termination-conversation script and delivery-day choreography (HR + manager + witness, meeting length under 15 minutes, empathy + finality + clarity); the severance-and-release agreement (post-Speak Out Act 2022 sexual-harassment / assault carve-outs, OWBPA-compliant age-40+ release, non-disparagement / mutual-non-disparagement); and the final-pay / COBRA mechanics.
6. **The RIF (Reduction in Force) / layoff playbook under WARN.** Board authorisation of the RIF decision; the impact analysis (function / level / demographic / cost-savings); WARN Act compliance (federal 60-day notice for 100+ employees with 50+ affected, plus stricter state mini-WARN in California, New York, New Jersey, and Wisconsin); OWBPA-compliant demographic disclosure for age-40+ affected employees; the executive-communications choreography (all-hands announcement, individual-conversation scheduling within 24–48 hours); the severance / benefits / outplacement package; and the anti-signalling operating norms during and after a layoff.
7. **The executive-team offboarding playbook.** The CEO-executive difficult-conversation script (Horowitz / Hoffman canon); the board-communication choreography (board notice before executive announcement, board-decision authorisation when material); the exec-severance-and-release execution (with attention to material board-decision items — CoC acceleration triggers, indemnification continuation, board-seat resignation if applicable); and the employee-communications playbook for an executive departure.

An eighth problem-space closes the loop:

8. **Alumni, rehire eligibility, and the exit-interview feedback loop.** The alumni-network programme, the exit-interview data-collection loop that closes back into the performance-and-management improvement backlog, and the rehire-eligibility decision framework.

Get this operating system right and the corporation compounds — good performers get seen and promoted, weak performers get coached or exited with dignity, PIPs recover the majority of the people they are opened on, terminations do not become EEOC charges, layoffs do not trigger a state-attorney-general WARN action, executive departures do not spook the market or the board, and alumni become the strongest single source of both re-hires and referrals. Get it wrong and the corporation limps — top performers leave because they cannot see a path, weak performers stay because no one wants to have the conversation, PIPs are used as a paper trail and everyone knows it, a Colorado / California / New York termination package skips the state-specific release wording and gets rescinded, a 55-person layoff in California triggers a Cal-WARN violation because the corporation counted federal-WARN thresholds instead, the CFO exits without a board consent and the audit committee finds out on LinkedIn.

## Ownership boundary (quick reference)

This module owns the **general performance / promotion / development / offboarding infrastructure**. It hands off:

- **Equity-compensation policy on separation payments** (severance-plus-acceleration policy, CoC-trigger interaction, post-termination exercise windows) → [mod-105 — Equity-Compensation Policy & the Compensation Committee](../mod-105-equity-compensation-policy-and-comp-committee/).
- **Promotion-comp band progression and merit-budget allocation** (what a promotion costs in cash and equity, how it lands in the [annual comp cycle](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/04-annual-comp-cycle.md)) → [mod-106 — Compensation Architecture & Total Rewards](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/).
- **Culture / engagement / DEI programme design** (values operationalisation, engagement measurement, sponsorship, retention) → [mod-108 — Culture, Employee Experience & DEI](../mod-108-culture-employee-experience-and-dei/).
- **GTM-role-specific PIP and performance nuance** (quota-attainment thresholds as PIP triggers, ramp-fail vs. capability-fail distinction, sales-manager coaching cadence) → `startup-product-gtm-curriculum`.
- **Engineering-role-specific performance and promotion nuance** (E1 → E7 competency ladders, staff-plus promotion criteria, tech-lead vs. manager track) → `cto-curriculum`.
- **Transaction-related workforce reductions** (M&A-integration-driven RIFs, buyer-side retention pools, transaction-close severance mechanics) → `startup-exit-curriculum`.

See [chapter 09](./09-ownership-boundary-map.md) for the full boundary map.

## What "done" looks like

By the end of this module you can, for a hypothetical multi-stage company, produce and defend:

1. A performance-management operating system — a semi-annual formal review cadence calendared to the comp cycle, a review-format decision by level, calibration norms, a manager-training curriculum, and a tool selection.
2. A promotion architecture — a promotion-committee construction, promotion criteria per level, a cadence, a promotion-communication playbook, and a not-promoted-signal-management playbook.
3. A development / growth-plan / L&D programme — a semi-annual growth conversation instrument, an IDP template, a manager-coaching programme, an L&D budget allocation, an L&D-tool selection, and an internal-mobility programme.
4. A PIP mechanics playbook — trigger criteria, a 30 / 60 / 90-day structure, weekly check-in template, a conclusion decision framework, and the anti-abuse operating norms.
5. A voluntary-and-involuntary termination playbook — an exit-interview instrument, an involuntary-decision framework, a termination-conversation script, a Speak-Out-Act-compliant separation-and-release template, an OWBPA-compliant age-40+ release template, and a final-pay / COBRA runbook.
6. A WARN-compliant RIF playbook — the board-authorisation memo, the impact-analysis instrument, federal-WARN and state-mini-WARN determination, the OWBPA disclosure schedule for the age-40+ pool, the comms choreography, the package design, and the anti-signalling operating norms.
7. An executive-team offboarding playbook — the CEO-executive difficult-conversation script, the board-communication choreography, the exec-severance-and-release execution runbook, and the employee-communications template.
8. An alumni and rehire-eligibility programme — the alumni-network operating model, the exit-interview feedback loop, and the rehire-eligibility decision framework.
9. A defensible ownership-boundary map — "does this performance / promotion / development / offboarding question belong here, in mod-105, mod-106, mod-108, in the GTM track, in the CTO track, or in the exit track?"
