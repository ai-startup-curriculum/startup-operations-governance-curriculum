# mod-108 — Culture, Employee Experience & DEI

> Values and anti-values, an NLRB-safe handbook, a post-SFFA DEI programme, an engagement loop that actually closes, a deliberate remote / hybrid / RTO posture, an AI acceptable-use policy, and an internal-comms operating rhythm — the people-ops policy layer that turns the mechanics of hiring, comp, and performance into a corporation that someone actually wants to work at.

**Track:** Startup Operations & Governance (level 50) · **Stage:** SEED / SERIES-A / SERIES-B / GROWTH · **Pillar:** people-ops · **Hours:** 32

## Why this module exists

[mod-103](../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/) authored the employment contract, the at-will / arbitration / non-compete / non-solicit architecture, and the state-law variance underneath. [mod-104](../mod-104-hiring-onboarding-and-hr-operations/) stood up the HRIS / PEO stack and the hiring-and-onboarding loop. [mod-105](../mod-105-equity-compensation-policy-and-comp-committee/) wrote the equity-compensation policy and seated the compensation committee. [mod-106](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/) built the levels, bands, geographic-pay strategy, and the annual comp cycle. [mod-107](../mod-107-performance-promotion-and-offboarding/) wired performance, promotion, PIPs, and the WARN-compliant RIF playbook. What none of those modules did is answer the question a CEO, a board, and every new employee eventually ask: *what is it actually like to work here, and how do we know?*

This module owns the policy layer that answers it.

Culture / employee-experience / DEI decomposes into seven operating decisions that have to be made on purpose rather than by drift:

1. **Values, behaviours, and anti-values.** A selection framework that keeps "innovation / integrity / teamwork" platitudes off the list; behaviourally-anchored specifications a hiring rubric and a review calibration can score against; explicitly-named anti-values; and the integration that lands values in hiring, review, recognition, and offboarding so they are load-bearing rather than decorative.
2. **The employee handbook.** A document structured for NLRA Section 7 safety under the Stericycle (2023) framework and the McLaren Macomb (2023) severance-and-release doctrine, layered with a state-and-local supplement matrix, versioned on a documented cadence, and re-acknowledged often enough to stay enforceable. The handbook *houses* the clauses that [mod-103](../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/) actually drafts and [mod-104](../mod-104-hiring-onboarding-and-hr-operations/) actually administers through the HRIS / PEO.
3. **The DEI programme in the post-SFFA landscape.** A portfolio redesign — fellowships, sponsorship, supplier programmes, hiring-panel norms, pay-equity audit, ERGs — rebuilt to survive Title VII, section 1981, and Muldrow / Ames scrutiny while still moving the demographic and inclusion outcomes the corporation cares about.
4. **The engagement-measurement programme.** A calendared loop of quarterly pulse, annual deep-dive, onboarding pulse, and exit / alumni surveys — with the executive-response and manager-cascade discipline that keeps response rates from collapsing into survey theatre.
5. **The remote / hybrid / RTO operating policy.** A deliberate location posture (remote-first / hybrid-by-default / office-first / RTO-with-exceptions), wired to the geographic-pay strategy in [mod-106](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/), collaboration norms, in-person cadence, tool stack, and an RTO-shift framework with a defensible notice period and accommodation process.
6. **AI usage and acceptable-use policy.** A tiered tool framework, a data-class matrix, a human-in-the-loop requirement for shipped output, a disclosure architecture, and a progressive-discipline path — the employee-behaviour policy that keeps the corporation from absorbing confidentiality, IP, and regulator-disclosure exposure through unregulated shadow-AI use.
7. **The internal-communications operating rhythm.** A calendared cadence of all-hands, CEO letters, AMAs, and skip-levels; an announcement taxonomy; a confidential-communications norm for board decisions, executive changes, layoffs, and MNPI; a cross-time-zone inclusive pattern; and a crisis-comms playbook.

An eighth problem-space closes the loop:

8. **The ownership-boundary map.** The reference that decides — for any culture / handbook / DEI / engagement / RTO / AI-usage / internal-comms question — whether it belongs in mod-108, in a sibling module, or in a non-role track. Values operationalisation inside engineering belongs to `cto-curriculum`; sales-org quota-culture nuance belongs to `startup-product-gtm-curriculum`; AI model-risk, red-teaming, and evaluation methodology belong to `chief-ai-officer-learning` / `head-of-ai-governance-learning` / `ai-risk-engineer-learning`. HR-data privacy flows to [mod-110](../mod-110-privacy-data-governance-and-sector-compliance/); the board-level DE&I-committee charter lives in [mod-111](../mod-111-corporate-governance-board-operations-and-officer-duties/); and the public-company human-capital-management disclosure that this module's artefacts eventually feed belongs to [mod-114](../mod-114-operations-function-design/) once the corporation is reporting under Reg S-K Item 101(c).

Get this right and the corporation compounds — values that interview panels actually score against, a handbook that survives an NLRB regional-office read, a DEI programme that moves metrics without inviting a section-1981 preliminary injunction, an engagement loop employees still believe in by cycle three, an RTO posture that does not produce a surprise retention cliff, an AI-usage policy that lets the workforce move fast without pasting customer contracts into consumer chatbots, and an all-hands rhythm the workforce reads as "we are being told the things that can be told." Get it wrong and the corporation absorbs the predictable failure set — "value violation" terminations with no documented behaviour, a 2018-template confidentiality clause that draws an unfair-labor-practice charge, a race-restricted fellowship that gets enjoined, a survey response rate that falls from 82% to 41% across three cycles of executive silence, an unannounced RTO that moves regretted attrition into the caregiving and disabled pools, an engineer summarising a customer contract in personal ChatGPT, and a disappearing all-hands that quietly drifts from weekly to monthly to never.

## Ownership boundary (quick reference)

This module owns the **people-ops policy layer** — values, handbook structure, DEI programme design, engagement measurement, RTO posture, AI acceptable-use rules, and the internal-comms rhythm. It hands off:

- **The employment contract and its clause-level enforceability** (at-will, arbitration, non-compete, non-solicit, PIIA, state-law variance) → [mod-103 — Employment Law, Worker Classification & Contract Design](../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/). The handbook cites these; mod-103 drafts and defends them.
- **The HRIS / PEO handbook-touchpoint and acknowledgment mechanics** (how the handbook is distributed, acknowledged, versioned, and audited inside the people-ops stack) → [mod-104 — Hiring, Onboarding & HR Operations](../mod-104-hiring-onboarding-and-hr-operations/).
- **The compensation-committee governance of any pay-equity remediation and the executive-comp equity decisions it touches** → [mod-105 — Equity-Compensation Policy & the Compensation Committee](../mod-105-equity-compensation-policy-and-comp-committee/).
- **The levels / bands / geographic-pay strategy that the RTO posture pins and the merit-cycle mechanic that lands pay-equity remediation** → [mod-106 — Compensation Architecture & Total Rewards](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/).
- **The individual-manager and individual-employee response to engagement-survey findings — PIP paths, promotion-committee treatment, exit-interview feedback into performance** → [mod-107 — Performance, Promotion & Offboarding](../mod-107-performance-promotion-and-offboarding/).
- **The privacy / data-governance regime that governs what HR, engagement-survey, and AI-usage data can flow where** → [mod-110 — Privacy, Data Governance & Sector Compliance](../mod-110-privacy-data-governance-and-sector-compliance/).
- **The board-level DE&I-committee charter, officer-fiduciary-duty constraints on CEO all-hands speech during a live deal or investigation, and the board-decks / executive-session side of confidential comms** → [mod-111 — Corporate Governance, Board Operations & Officer Duties](../mod-111-corporate-governance-board-operations-and-officer-duties/).
- **The public-company human-capital-management disclosure (Reg S-K Item 101(c)) that this module's artefacts eventually feed, alongside the WBR / QBR / board-pack operating cadence that runs parallel to the culture-comms rhythm** → [mod-114 — Operations Function Design](../mod-114-operations-function-design/).

Non-role curriculum tracks also carry pieces this module explicitly defers:

- `chief-ai-officer-learning`, `head-of-ai-governance-learning`, `ai-risk-engineer-learning` — AI model-risk methodology, red-teaming programme design, and evaluation-suite construction. The AI-usage chapter sets *employee-behaviour rules*; the model-risk substrate lives in these tracks.
- `startup-product-gtm-curriculum` — sales-org-specific values operationalisation, quota-culture nuance, and sales-manager coaching cadence on culture signals.
- `cto-curriculum` — engineering-culture-specific values operationalisation (code-review culture, on-call culture, blameless post-mortems, tech-writing norms).

See [chapter 08](./08-ownership-boundary-map.md) for the full boundary map and the eight boundary worked examples.

## What "done" looks like

By the end of this module you can, for a hypothetical multi-stage company, produce and defend:

1. A values-and-anti-values pack — four-test-screened candidate values with behaviour anchors, trade-off paragraphs, and the matched anti-values list, wired into the hiring rubric, the review calibration, the peer-recognition programme, and the offboarding posture.
2. An employee handbook — chapter list, Stericycle-narrowed workplace rules, McLaren-Macomb-compliant severance-and-release template, state-and-local supplement matrix, and the update-and-re-acknowledgment cadence.
3. A post-SFFA DEI programme — the audited portfolio across fellowships / sponsorship / supplier programmes / hiring-panel norms / ERGs, the pay-equity audit methodology, the remediation trigger that hands off to [mod-106](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/) and [mod-105](../mod-105-equity-compensation-policy-and-comp-committee/), and the comms posture for the shift.
4. An engagement-measurement programme — the four-survey portfolio with cadences, the vendor selection, the stable anchor-question set, the publish-fast / executive-response / manager-cascade action-planning loop, and the small-team anonymity-threshold rule.
5. A remote / hybrid / RTO operating policy — the chosen posture, the geographic-pay-strategy wiring, the collaboration norms and in-person cadence, the tool stack, and the RTO-shift framework with notice period and documented accommodation-exception process.
6. An AI usage and acceptable-use policy — the four-tier tool registry, the data-class matrix, the human-in-the-loop and disclosure architecture, and the progressive-discipline path aligned to [mod-107](../mod-107-performance-promotion-and-offboarding/).
7. An internal-communications operating rhythm — the stage-appropriate all-hands cadence, the executive-communication cadence, the AMA pattern, the announcement taxonomy, the confidential-communications norm, the cross-time-zone inclusive pattern, and the crisis-comms playbook.
8. A defensible ownership-boundary map — "does this culture / handbook / DEI / engagement / RTO / AI-usage / internal-comms question belong here, in a sibling module ([mod-103](../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/), [mod-104](../mod-104-hiring-onboarding-and-hr-operations/), [mod-105](../mod-105-equity-compensation-policy-and-comp-committee/), [mod-106](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/), [mod-107](../mod-107-performance-promotion-and-offboarding/), [mod-110](../mod-110-privacy-data-governance-and-sector-compliance/), [mod-111](../mod-111-corporate-governance-board-operations-and-officer-duties/), [mod-114](../mod-114-operations-function-design/)), or in a non-role track (`chief-ai-officer-learning`, `head-of-ai-governance-learning`, `ai-risk-engineer-learning`, `startup-product-gtm-curriculum`, `cto-curriculum`)?"
