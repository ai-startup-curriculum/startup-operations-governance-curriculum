# Resources — mod-102 Founding-Team Legal Architecture

Primary statutes, regulations, agency publications, case law, and standard-form references cited across the chapters. Prefer these to secondary summaries.

## Internal Revenue Code and IRS publications

- **IRC § 83** — property transferred in connection with performance of services.
  - § 83(a) — general timing rule (income recognised at vesting).
  - § 83(b) — election to include in income at grant.
- **Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2** — mechanics of the § 83(b) election, including the 30-day deadline and the required-elements list at § 1.83-2(e). https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/section-1.83-2
- **Rev. Proc. 2012-29** — sample § 83(b) election language. https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-12-29.pdf
- **IRS Form 15620** — standardised § 83(b) election form (introduced by IRS in 2024). <!-- needs-research: confirm current-year IRS guidance on Form 15620 status (mandatory vs. optional vs. supplementary to firm-drafted variants). -->
- **IRC § 1202** — Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS). <!-- needs-research: § 1202 was amended by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-XX, July 2025); confirm the current gross-asset cap, per-issuer exclusion cap, and tiered-holding-period rules before quoting numbers. -->
- **IRC § 409A** — deferred compensation; underlies the 409A valuation regime for stock-option and post-formation restricted-stock grants (mod-105 depth).
- **IRC § 422** — Incentive stock options (ISOs); § 422(b)(1) 12-month stockholder-approval window.
- **IRC § 7502(f) and IRS Notice 2016-30** — approved private-delivery services for tax filings. https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-16-30.pdf
- **IRS Publication 15-A** — Employer's Supplemental Tax Guide (worker-classification common-law test). https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p15a.pdf
- **IRS "Where to File Paper Tax Returns" list** — the correct IRS Service Center address for a paper § 83(b) election is the same as the taxpayer's Form 1040 filing address. https://www.irs.gov/filing/where-to-file-paper-tax-returns-with-or-without-a-payment

## Federal intellectual-property statutes

- **17 U.S.C. § 101** — "Work made for hire" definition (Copyright Act). https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/101
- **17 U.S.C. § 201(b)** — Copyright ownership of works made for hire.
- **18 U.S.C. §§ 1836–1839** — Defend Trade Secrets Act (DTSA).
  - **§ 1833(b)(1)–(2)** — whistleblower immunities.
  - **§ 1833(b)(3)** — notice requirement to employees (and contractors, per § 1833(b)(4)).
  - **§ 1836(b)** — federal civil cause of action; remedy stack including exemplary damages up to 2× compensatory (§ 1836(b)(3)(C)) and attorneys' fees (§ 1836(b)(3)(D)).
  - **§ 1839(3)** — trade-secret definition, including "reasonable measures."
- **35 U.S.C. § 261** — assignments of patents (context for *Stanford v. Roche*'s present-assignment holding).

## Delaware and Federal case law

- **Stanford v. Roche Molecular Systems, Inc.**, 583 F.3d 832 (Fed. Cir. 2009), aff'd on other grounds, 563 U.S. 776 (2011) — "hereby assigns" vs. "agrees to assign" present-assignment holding. https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/10pdf/09-1159.pdf
- **Community for Creative Non-Violence v. Reid**, 490 U.S. 730 (1989) — employee-vs-contractor distinction under 17 U.S.C. § 101 (Copyright Act). https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/490/730
- **In re Caremark International Inc. Derivative Litigation**, 698 A.2d 959 (Del. Ch. 1996) — director oversight duty.
- **Marchand v. Barnhill**, 212 A.3d 805 (Del. 2019) — reinvigoration of *Caremark*; mission-critical-risk oversight.
- **In re Clovis Oncology, Inc. Derivative Litigation**, 2019 WL 4850188 (Del. Ch. Oct. 1, 2019) — *Caremark* claim survived motion to dismiss on failure-to-monitor mission-critical FDA regulatory risk.
- **In re Boeing Co. Derivative Litigation**, 2021 WL 4059934 (Del. Ch. Sept. 7, 2021) — *Caremark* claim survived on mission-critical airline-safety oversight.
- **Dynamex Operations West, Inc. v. Superior Court**, 4 Cal. 5th 903 (2018) — ABC test for worker classification (California).
- **Kahn v. M&F Worldwide Corp.**, 88 A.3d 635 (Del. 2014) — controlling-stockholder framework (procedural safeguards). Relevance grows in later modules (mod-111).
- **Corwin v. KKR Financial Holdings LLC**, 125 A.3d 304 (Del. 2015) — cleansing effect of fully-informed disinterested-stockholder vote.
