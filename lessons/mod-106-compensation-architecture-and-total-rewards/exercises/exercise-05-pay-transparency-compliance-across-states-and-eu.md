# Exercise 05 — Pay transparency compliance across states and EU

> Estimated time: **~5 hours** · Related chapter: [05 — Pay-transparency compliance across states and the EU](../05-pay-transparency-compliance.md)

## Problem statement

Threadspire Medical, Inc. is a Delaware C-corp building a clinical-workflow and remote-patient-monitoring platform for mid-sized health systems. The corporation closed a $75M Series-B seven months ago and is operating across a multi-state US footprint plus a nascent EU bridgehead. Current headcount is **182 employees**:

- **United States (164)** — distributed across CA (26), CO (14), WA (12), NY (28), NJ (6), IL (11), MA (9), TX (18), GA (8), MN (7), OH (5), with a tier-2 / tier-3 remote long tail (20).
- **Ireland (12)** — a Dublin engineering and clinical-operations office, incorporated as Threadspire Medical EU Ltd., actively hiring.
- **Germany (6)** — a Berlin sales and clinical-partnerships bridgehead, employed through an EOR while a GmbH is in the works.

Current state of the pay-transparency posture:

- **No ratified pay-transparency compliance plan.** Postings go up on Greenhouse (the ATS) with pay ranges populated by the recruiting coordinator based on what the hiring manager tells them "the band is" — a number that in multiple cases is not the ratified band from the Head of Total Rewards's band file ([exercise 02](./exercise-02-cash-and-equity-band-authoring-by-geography.md)).
- **Three live compliance incidents** the GC and Head of Total Rewards have flagged in the last 90 days:
    1. **A Colorado posting for a Senior Clinical Operations Manager** omitted the pay range for three business days before a candidate flagged it. The Division of Labor has not raised a complaint, but the corporation's employment counsel has advised remediation in writing and voluntary self-reporting is being considered.
    2. **A New York State posting for a Senior Backend Engineer** carried a "$150,000 – $350,000" range the corporation cannot defend as a good-faith range. A candidate commented publicly on LinkedIn; the post has 180 reposts. The NY State DOL has not opened a complaint but the Office of the Attorney General has an active industry-wide sweep.
    3. **A Dublin role for a Clinical Data Engineer** posted on Threadspire's careers page carries no pay range at all. The corporation's view has been that Ireland is not yet a pay-transparency jurisdiction; the GC has pushed back that the EU Pay Transparency Directive (Directive (EU) 2023/970) transposition deadline is 2026-06-07 and that Ireland is on track to transpose by then.
- **A compensation-cycle communications gap.** In the Q1 2026 cycle, two employees filed internal complaints alleging the pay statements they received were "non-transparent" and that the manager-script dodged the "what is the band for my level?" question. No formal grievance has been filed with an outside body, but the issue has reached the compensation committee's attention.
- **A Series-C process targeted in Q4 2026.** The lead investor's operating partner has pre-flagged that the Severity-2 finding from Series-B diligence ("no documented pay-transparency compliance plan, no state-specific posting workflow, no EU-directive readiness timeline") must be closed before diligence re-engages.

Your role: you are the **Head of Total Rewards** (working with the General Counsel) tasked with authoring the ratifiable pay-transparency compliance plan that addresses the three live incidents, remediates the compensation-cycle communications gap, builds the state-and-country posting workflow, and closes the EU-directive readiness timeline. Produce the compliance-state matrix, the posting workflow, the comp-cycle-communications upgrade, the EU Pay Transparency Directive readiness plan, the three-incident remediation, and the comp-committee presentation memo.

### Part B — Posting workflow

Produce the posting workflow that integrates with the ATS (Greenhouse) and the band file. Cover:

1. **The band-to-posting reconciliation gate.** The rule that every posting carries the band from the ratified band file (from [exercise 02](./exercise-02-cash-and-equity-band-authoring-by-geography.md)) — not a hiring-manager-estimated number. State who owns the reconciliation (the Head of Total Rewards or a delegated approver), the lead time required before the recruiting coordinator posts, and the exception path (for genuinely new roles whose band has not been authored yet — the band is authored *before* the posting, not after).
2. **The multi-state-posting rule.** The rule for whether a single posting lists one range (national / tier-1-anchored) or multiple ranges (per tier) when the role is open to multiple geographies. Chapter 05 notes the tension — a single range is simpler to post but may violate a CO / NY statute if the range posted is not the one applicable to a CO / NY applicant. State the chosen rule for Threadspire with the rationale.
3. **The good-faith-range definition.** State the specific good-faith-range rule the corporation will apply: the range reflects what the corporation would actually pay the successful applicant at the level the role is being posted at, with floor and ceiling anchored to the ratified band and a width no wider than the ratified band. State the specific exception — above-ceiling scarcity-skill-premium posts are reviewed by the Head of Total Rewards *before* posting, with a documented premium that justifies the posted ceiling.
4. **The posting-template library.** A template per jurisdiction with the statute-specific language (pay range, benefits summary, bonus and equity disclosure where required). Specifically address the CO EPEWA bonus-and-equity disclosure requirement, the NY State S9427A description-of-benefits language, and the IL HB 3129 benefits-summary language. Flag `<!-- needs-research -->` for specific statutory-language cuts.
5. **The posting-review audit.** A quarterly audit the Head of Total Rewards runs against the posted ranges — pull every live posting, check the posted range against the ratified band, document any variance, and route any variance to a remediation conversation. Cross-reference [chapter 04](../04-annual-comp-cycle.md) for the cycle-integration.

### Part C — Comp-cycle communications upgrade

Produce the communications upgrade that closes the Q1 2026 cycle complaints. Cover:

1. **The compensation-statement template.** The template Marigold-style written compensation statement (`<!-- needs-research: reconcile with mod-106 exercise 04 Part D -->`) that includes current base, new base, bonus target and 2025 actual, equity refresh, and *the band range for the employee's level in the employee's geography*. State the policy decision — the band range *is* disclosed to the employee on the statement — and the trade-off accepted (the employee learns peer-band information whether they ask for it or not).
2. **The manager-script upgrade.** The upgraded manager script for the "what is the band?" and "am I paid fairly?" questions. 3. **The NLRA § 7 backdrop.** State the explicit NLRA § 7 (29 USC § 157) protected-concerted-activity backdrop — employees have a federal right to discuss pay; the corporation cannot retaliate against that discussion and cannot require NDAs that prohibit pay discussion. Cross-reference [mod-103](../../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/) for the full NLRA treatment.
4. **The two internal complaints.** For each of the two Q1 complaints, state the specific remediation — the pay statement re-issued with the band, the manager re-trained with the upgraded script, the HRBP escalation documented in the HRIS — and the reporting line to the comp committee.

### Part D — EU Pay Transparency Directive readiness plan

1. **The directive's core obligations.** Enumerate the directive's six core obligations as they apply to Threadspire's EU footprint: pay-transparency in job postings and candidate conversations (Article 5), prohibition on asking candidates for pay history (Article 5(2)), employee right to information on pay levels and pay progression criteria (Article 7), gender-pay-gap reporting at ≥100-worker and ≥150-worker thresholds (Article 9), joint pay assessment at >5% unexplained gap (Article 10), and remedies / burden-shifting (Articles 14–17). Flag `<!-- needs-research -->` for the specific article-by-article cut.
2. **The transposition deadline.** Note the 2026-06-07 transposition deadline. For Ireland and Germany, flag `<!-- needs-research -->` for the current transposition status — has each member state passed the implementing legislation, and are the specific implementation details final? Note that transposition dates are legally required but member states have historically run late.
3. **The Threadspire readiness timeline.** A dated timeline for Threadspire's EU readiness:
    - **Pre-2026-06-07 (now through transposition).** Specific actions — update EU postings to carry pay ranges and end pay-history questions; author the pay-progression-criteria document the directive requires; pre-run the gender-pay-gap analysis for Ireland (12 workers, below the ≥100 threshold today but on a track to cross).
    - **Post-2026-06-07 (compliance window).** Specific actions — formal pay-information-on-request workflow, gender-pay-gap reporting readiness (even below threshold, to demonstrate compliance posture), joint-pay-assessment preparedness.
    - **Watch-points.** The Ireland transposition legislation's specific detail that affects Threadspire's operating model — in particular the >5% unexplained gap trigger and the joint-pay-assessment procedure.
4. **The job-architecture tie-in.** The directive requires "objective, gender-neutral criteria used to evaluate and compare roles." State the tie-in to Threadspire's ratified job architecture (from [exercise 01](./exercise-01-job-architecture-and-leveling-framework-authoring.md)) — the leveling rubric *is* the objective-criteria artifact, and the Head of Total Rewards' master document is the publication-ready version.
5. **The Germany EOR-to-GmbH migration interaction.** The German entity migration from EOR to GmbH interacts with the directive's employer-of-record attribution. Cross-reference [mod-113](../../mod-113-international-expansion-and-global-workforce/) for the EOR-to-subsidiary playbook.

- The three live incidents are the comp committee's anchor cases. Treat them as test cases for the Part A / B / D framework rather than one-off remediations.
- **Do not manufacture statute language or EU-directive cuts.** Every statute citation, every civil-penalty amount, every retention window, every member-state transposition detail must be flagged `<!-- needs-research -->` with the specific statute, the current legislative session, the regulation or guidance cited, and the vintage.
- The EU-directive readiness plan is the forward-looking anchor. The directive's transposition deadline is 2026-06-07; the readiness window is the next several months. Treat Part D as the hinge document for the EU bridgehead.
- The NLRA § 7 backdrop in Part C is a federal floor, not a state-specific nuance. Cite it explicitly in the manager-script upgrade and in the compensation-statement template.
- The posting-template library in Part B defers specific statutory language to the statute and to employment counsel. Do not invent the statutory language — reference the statute and flag with `<!-- needs-research -->`.
