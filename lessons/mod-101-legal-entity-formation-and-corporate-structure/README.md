# mod-101 — Legal Entity Formation & Corporate Structure

> Delaware C-Corp, foreign qualification, and the corporate record.

**Track:** Startup Operations & Governance (level 50) · **Stage:** IDEA / PRE-SEED · **Pillar:** legal · **Hours:** 22

## Why this module exists

The first act of a venture-backed startup is not a product — it is the entity that will one day sign a term sheet, hire employees, take money, and (eventually) close a Series-A financing whose diligence checklist assumes the entity was stood up correctly on day one. Sloppy formation is not "harmless paperwork"; it is a discovery on the schedule of exceptions two years later, a tax bill on a founder's vested stock, or a re-issuance of the entire cap table under Series-A counsel's supervision.

This module owns the *entity* layer. It teaches the incoming COO, GC, or corporate-secretary hire how to (a) pick the right entity, (b) stand it up correctly, (c) maintain the corporate record that Series-A diligence will read, (d) foreign-qualify wherever the company has presence, (e) design subsidiaries when a real need appears, and (f) diagnose and clean up the corporate record it will almost always inherit in shambles.

## What "done" looks like

By the end of this module you can, for a hypothetical founding team, produce and defend:

1. An entity-choice decision memo (DE C-Corp default, alternatives considered, why).
2. A filed Certificate of Incorporation and adopted Bylaws.
3. A signed initial board consent covering director / officer appointments, bylaws / stock-plan adoption, and founders' issuances.
4. A foreign-qualification matrix across the states of operation with penalty exposure quantified.
5. A signed founders' stock purchase agreement with vesting, cliff, and a mailed 83(b).
6. A compliance calendar covering the DE franchise tax, CA / relevant-state annual reports, and registered-agent renewals.
7. A Series-A-grade corporate record cleanup plan for an existing, messy corporate book.
