  - § 102 — Certificate of Incorporation contents; § 102(b)(7) — director (and, post-2022 amendment, officer) exculpation.
  - § 109 — Bylaws.
  - § 132 — Registered office and registered agent.
  - § 141 — Board of directors; § 141(f) — action by unanimous written consent.
  - § 142 — Officers.
  - § 145 — Indemnification of officers, directors, employees and agents.
  - § 151 — Classes and series of stock; blank-check preferred.
  - § 152 — Issuance of stock; consideration.
  - § 158 — Uncertificated shares.
  - § 211 — Meetings of stockholders (§ 211(b) — annual meeting requirement).
  - § 220 — Books and records; stockholder inspection right.
  - § 223 — Vacancies on the board.
  - § 224 — Form of records; stock ledger requirement.
  - § 228 — Stockholder action by written consent.
  - § 265 — Conversion of other entities to a domestic corporation.

## Internal Revenue Code and IRS publications

- **IRC § 83** — property transferred in connection with performance of services.
  - § 83(a) — general timing rule (income at vesting).
  - § 83(b) — election to include in income at grant.
  - **Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2** — mechanics and 30-day deadline. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/section-1.83-2
  - **Rev. Proc. 2012-29** — sample § 83(b) election language. https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-12-29.pdf
- **IRC § 422** — Incentive stock options (ISOs); § 422(b)(1) 12-month stockholder-approval window.
- **IRC § 1202** — Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS). <!-- needs-research: § 1202 was amended by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-XX, July 2025); confirm the current gross-asset cap, per-issuer exclusion cap, and tiered-holding-period rules before quoting numbers. -->
- **IRC § 1361** — S corporation eligibility rules (≤100 shareholders, single class of stock, eligible shareholder categories).
- **IRC § 1362** — S corporation election.
- **IRC § 6039** — information reporting; Form 3921 (ISO exercises) and Form 3922 (ESPP transfers). Form and instructions: https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-3921 · https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-3922
- **IRS Form SS-4** — Application for Employer Identification Number. https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-ss-4
- **IRS Form 1120** — U.S. Corporation Income Tax Return. https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1120

## Foreign-qualification statutes (representative)

- **California Corporations Code** — https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codesTOCSelected.xhtml?tocCode=CORP
  - § 191 — activities not constituting "doing intrastate business" (safe harbours).
  - § 2100 et seq. — foreign corporations; § 2105 — application for Certificate of Qualification.
  - § 2203 — penalties for transacting intrastate business without qualification (per-day monetary penalty, denial of court access).
- **California Franchise Tax Board** — corporate franchise tax; Form 100. https://www.ftb.ca.gov/
- **California Secretary of State — Statement of Information (SI-550).** https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/statements/
- **New York Business Corporation Law (B.C.L.)** — https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/BSC
  - § 1301 — doing business in NY by foreign corporations.
  - § 1304 — application for authority (including publication requirement).
  - § 1312 — action by unauthorised foreign corporation (denial of court access).
- **New York Department of State — Division of Corporations.** https://dos.ny.gov/division-corporations
- **Texas Business Organizations Code** — https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/BO/htm/BO.9.htm
  - § 9.001 — registration to transact business.
  - § 9.051 / § 9.052 — late-registration penalties.
- **Texas Comptroller — Franchise Tax and Public Information Report.** https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/
- **Massachusetts General Laws (M.G.L.) c. 156D** — Business Corporations Act (§ 15.02 penalty for unauthorised doing business).
- **Washington RCW 23.95** — Uniform Business Organizations Code (§ 23.95.505 penalty for unauthorised doing business).
- **Illinois Business Corporation Act** — 805 ILCS 5 (§ 13.70 penalty).
- **Florida Business Corporation Act** — Fla. Stat. § 607.1502 penalty.
- **Model Business Corporation Act** § 15.01 — "doing business" safe harbours (adopted by many states).
