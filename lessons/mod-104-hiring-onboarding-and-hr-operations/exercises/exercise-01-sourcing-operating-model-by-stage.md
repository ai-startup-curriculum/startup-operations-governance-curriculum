# Exercise 01 — Sourcing operating model by stage

> Estimated time: **~4 hours** · Related chapter: [01 — The sourcing operating model by stage](../01-sourcing-operating-model-by-stage.md)

## Problem statement

Northwind Systems is a Delaware C-corp building an AI-infra platform. It has 22 employees today (8 engineering, 5 product / design, 4 GTM including the founder-CEO, 3 G&A / operations, 2 research). Northwind just closed a $22M Series-A led by an established venture firm. The board-approved hiring plan is **22 → 60 by month 18** — 15 engineering hires, 4 product / design, 12 GTM including a VP Sales, 4 research, and 3 G&A / operations.

The founder-CEO has personally sourced 19 of the current 22 employees; the other 3 came from a fractional recruiter engaged briefly earlier this year at a monthly retainer, and one contingent recruiter engagement for a senior engineer that ended in a $46,250 fee (25% of first-year base of $185k). There is no ATS today — the founder tracks candidates in a spreadsheet, on LinkedIn Recruiter Lite, and in an email folder. No referral programme exists. The founder-CEO has been spending 22–28 hours per week on top-of-funnel sourcing, screening, and scheduling for the last two months and the CEO's other work — an outstanding product decision, a delayed enterprise-customer expansion, and two board asks — is visibly slipping.

Your role: you are the incoming Head of People (or an outside operating advisor asked by the CEO to design the sourcing operating model). Produce a staged sourcing operating model, a first-year budget, a graduation-trigger map, and a decision memo the CEO can walk the board through.

## Requirements

### Part A — Current-state diagnosis

Produce a written diagnosis of the current sourcing operating model. Cover:

1. **Which of the four canonical stages** (chapter 01) is Northwind currently operating in — cite the specific evidence (founder-hours per week, ATS discipline, referral programme, contingent-spend pattern).
2. **Which stage Northwind should be operating in** given the hiring plan and stage — and where the mismatch is.
3. **The three cost quantities** (founder time, cash, candidate signal) being traded today. For each, name the current spend / consumption and whether it is under, at, or over the appropriate level for a $22M-raise Series-A with a 22 → 60 plan.
4. **The three-to-five specific operating incidents** that are already surfacing or will surface within 90 days if nothing changes. Cite the chapter's specific failure patterns ("too much recruiter, too early"; "too little recruiter, too late"; "contingent fees, uncapped"; "retained exec search, mispriced") to name them.

### Part B — Target-state sourcing operating model

Design the target-state sourcing operating model Northwind should run for the next 12 months. Cover:

1. **Who sources** — the specific in-house roles (Head of Talent, additional recruiters, sourcers, coordinator) with hire-order and target start dates. Justify the sequencing against the chapter's Stage-2 / Stage-3 archetypes.
2. **Full-time vs. fractional split** — where fractional recruiters are used, for which roles, on what engagement terms (retainer vs. hourly, per-close bonus, guarantee), and with what graduation criteria.
3. **Contingent-recruiter policy** — the annual budget, per-seat cap, approval workflow above the cap, guarantee terms Northwind will insist on, and the specific role types where contingent will and will not be engaged.
4. **Retained exec-search policy** — for which role(s) in the 18-month plan retained search will be engaged (VP Sales is the obvious candidate; note whether the plan warrants any other), the fee-structure Northwind will insist on (one-third / one-third / one-third, guarantee, off-limits, exclusivity), and the shortlist of firms Northwind will interview.
5. **Referral programme** — the bonus schedule (IC / senior IC / manager / director tiers), payout timing (90-day anniversary), source-of-hire tracking, and the referral-participation target Northwind will manage against.
6. **Founder-CEO involvement** — the CEO's target sourcing / closing hours per week over the next 12 months, which roles the CEO stays in the loop for, and the specific hand-off from CEO-led to recruiter-led closing.
7. **Tooling** — the ATS, LinkedIn Recruiter tier, scheduling tool, and referral portal that support the plan. (Defer the deep ATS decision to exercise 02, but name what Northwind will pick.)

### Part C — First-year budget

Produce a line-item recruiting budget for the next 12 months. Each line has a description, an annualised cost (or a range where appropriate), and a note on how the spend scales if the plan slips (headcount misses) or accelerates (an unexpected exec search). Cover:

1. In-house TA-function total comp (Head of Talent, any additional recruiters or coordinator hired in the first 12 months).
2. Fractional-recruiter retainers.
3. Contingent-recruiter budget.
4. Retained exec-search fees (per-seat estimate for the VP Sales search plus any other exec search planned).
5. Referral bonuses (assumed hit-rate).
6. Tooling (ATS, LinkedIn Recruiter, scheduling, assessment, interview intelligence if applicable).
7. Miscellaneous (candidate travel, offer-package market data, employer-branding).

Total the budget. Compare it to the chapter's ~$15k / hire benchmark for a Series-A ($15k × 38 net-new hires = $570k). Explain any material deviation and justify it.

### Part D — Graduation-trigger map

Produce a written map of the graduation triggers Northwind will monitor for the next 12–24 months. For each of the four transitions the corporation may face (Stage 1 → 2, Stage 2 → 3, in-house-only → contingent overlay, Series-A recruiter-only → first exec-search engagement), specify:

1. The **quantitative or qualitative signal** that the trigger has fired (specific hours-per-week, time-to-fill drift, sourcer / recruiter ratio bottleneck, exec-search cadence — the chapter names these).
2. The **owner** who is watching for the signal (CEO, Head of Talent, board Head of People committee if applicable).
3. The **response** — what the corporation does within 30 / 60 / 90 days once the signal fires.

### Part E — Decision memo for the board

Produce a 2–3 page memo the founder-CEO can walk the board through. Cover:

- The current-state diagnosis (Part A distilled to three bullets).
- The target-state operating model (Part B distilled — the org chart, the fractional overlay, the exec-search intent).
- The 12-month budget (Part C's total plus the top three line items).
- The graduation-trigger map (Part D distilled).
- The two or three things the board specifically needs to know or approve — e.g., a comp band for the Head of Talent that exceeds the CEO's current base; a per-seat retained-search fee that exceeds a board-visible threshold; a founder-CEO commitment to reduce sourcing hours to a specified target.
- The `<!-- needs-research -->` markers you flagged and how the corporation will resolve them (current-market recruiter comp band, current contingent-fee band, current retained-search fee structure, current referral-bonus band, current tooling costs).

## Starter guidance

- Chapter 01 is the primary reference. The four-stage archetype and the "cost, cash, signal" tradeoff are laid out there.
- The chapter's worked example (a Series-A SaaS company with 22 employees hiring 33 more) is a close analogue — use it as a starting point rather than as a plug-and-play answer. Northwind's plan is larger (38 net-new vs. 33) and has a specific VP Sales exec-search need.
- Do not manufacture market numbers. Where you cite a comp band, a contingent-fee percentage, or a retained-search fee, use the chapter's ranges and flag them as `<!-- needs-research -->` items to refresh with current-market data.
- The Head of Talent hire is deliberately senior in the chapter's recommendation — this is a bet that the person will hire the next TA hires within 12 months. Explain why or why not you agree, and structure the role accordingly.
- The founder-CEO's current 22–28 hours/week of sourcing is above the chapter's ~15–20 hour graduation trigger. This is not a "consider hiring a recruiter someday" moment; this is the moment.
- The board's job on this decision is not to approve every hire; it is to bless the operating budget, the exec-search partner selection framework, and any board-visible spend (retained-search fees, senior-comp bands). Structure the memo accordingly.

## Acceptance criteria

The package is acceptable if:

1. The current-state diagnosis correctly identifies Northwind as operating in Stage 1 (founder-led) while needing Stage 2 (first in-house recruiter, possibly with fractional overlay) — with the specific evidence marshalled.
2. The target-state model names a specific hire (Head of Talent), a specific start-date target (within ~60 days of Series-A close), and a specific fractional overlay for GTM.
3. The retained exec-search policy identifies VP Sales as the exec-search candidate, names 2–4 firms to interview (from Heidrick / Spencer Stuart / True Search / Daversa / ON Partners / Bolster or a defensible substitute), and specifies the fee structure (one-third / one-third / one-third, guarantee period, off-limits period, exclusivity).
4. The contingent-recruiter policy names a specific annual budget, a per-seat cap, and a specific approval workflow above the cap.
5. The referral programme has a tiered bonus schedule, a 90-day-anniversary payout, and a specific referral-participation target.
6. The first-year budget totals to a defensible number, compares to the chapter's ~$15k / hire benchmark, and explains material deviations.
7. The graduation-trigger map has at least one *quantitative* signal per transition (hours-per-week, time-to-fill, sourcer / recruiter ratio, exec-search cadence) and names an owner and a response.
8. The board memo names at least three specific asks the board is being asked to bless.
9. Every market number cited (recruiter comp band, contingent-fee percentage, retained-search fee, referral bonus, tooling cost) is either taken directly from chapter 01's ranges or flagged with a `<!-- needs-research -->` marker.
10. Nothing left as `[TBD]` or `[FILL IN]`.
