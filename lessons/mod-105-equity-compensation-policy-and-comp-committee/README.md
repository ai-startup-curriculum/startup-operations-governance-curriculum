# mod-105 — Equity-Compensation Policy & the Compensation Committee

> Grant guidelines, the IC equity plan, refresh and promotion grants, exec packages, change-of-control acceleration, exec severance, Item 402 pre-IPO disclosure, and the compensation committee that ratifies all of it — the equity side of the offer, operated as policy rather than as one-off negotiation.

**Track:** Startup Operations & Governance (level 50) · **Stage:** SEED / SERIES-A / SERIES-B / GROWTH · **Pillar:** people-ops · **Hours:** 30

## Why this module exists

[mod-103](../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/) authored the employee-facing legal architecture — the offer letter, the PIIA, the FLSA classification, the arbitration-and-class-action-waiver pack. [mod-104](../mod-104-hiring-onboarding-and-hr-operations/) built the hiring pipeline and the HRIS / PEO stack that turns a headcount plan into a working workforce. Neither module answers the question this one owns: **for every grant, refresh, promotion, exec package, severance payout, and acceleration event the corporation issues, what is the policy, who ratifies it, and how is it documented so that the comp committee, the 409A appraiser, the acquirer's counsel, and the eventual S-1 drafting session all see the same answer?**

Equity compensation is where more quiet dollars move than in any other people-ops surface. A seed-stage founding-engineer grant is denominated in points of the fully-diluted cap table; an exec package at Series-B is a seven-piece instrument (base, bonus, equity, PSU overlay, severance, CoC acceleration, indemnification) where each piece is independently taxed and independently negotiable; a 280G cleanse at an acquisition can save or burn single-digit percentages of enterprise value; the S-1 Summary Compensation Table is built from decisions made in comp-committee minutes three years earlier. Operators who treat equity as a founder-by-founder judgement call pay for it later — at the Series-B diligence read-through, at the exec separation that produces a wrongful-termination claim with no documented release process, at the IPO drafting session where the CD&A cannot explain the exec grant history.

Get the policy layer right and the corporation hires executives with a defensible package, refreshes ICs on a repeatable cadence, retains the top performers through CoC transactions, passes Reg S-K Item 402 disclosure review, and gives the comp committee enough procedural record to earn back the business-judgment presumption under Delaware's entire-fairness doctrine. Get it wrong and the corporation over-grants early and under-grants late, discovers mid-cycle that the ISO $100k vesting-value limit (IRC § 422(d)) was never tracked, finds out at closing that single-trigger acceleration triggers a § 280G parachute-payment problem, and spends the first year post-IPO rewriting its exec-comp narrative in front of proxy advisors.

The module is also where **governance risk concentrates**. Executive compensation is the archetypal self-dealing transaction: the CEO is the fiduciary, the CEO's pay is the transaction, the directors the CEO recruited are the ratifying body. The compensation committee is the structural answer — an independent-leaning board subset with its own advisor, its own executive sessions, and its own minutes — and this module teaches how to design that committee from the Series-A "informal" posture through the three-independent-director pre-IPO reconstitution.

## Ownership boundary (quick reference)

This module owns the **equity-compensation policy substance and the compensation-committee operating infrastructure.** It hands off:

- **Equity-comp economics** (option-pool sizing, dilution math, 409A valuation methodology, Rule 701 limits, QSBS and 83(b) tax arithmetic) → `startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`.
- **Change-of-control transaction execution** (the § 280G cleanse mechanics, tender-offer execution, acquirer-stock-election mechanics, closing allocation) → `startup-exit-curriculum`. mod-105 keeps the *CoC equity policy*; the exit track owns the *transaction execution* when the CoC actually happens.
- **Offer-letter architecture, worker classification, and pay-transparency state law** → [mod-103 — Employment Law, Worker Classification & Contract Design](../mod-103-employment-law-and-contract-design/).
- **The executive-hiring pipeline** (retained-search selection, interview panel, offer presentation, 100-day plan) → [mod-104 — Hiring, Onboarding & HR Operations](../mod-104-hiring-onboarding-and-hr-operations/). mod-104 owns the *process* of hiring an executive; mod-105 owns the *policy substance* that fills in the offer.
- **Cash-comp architecture, leveling, bands, benchmarking, benefits design, pay-transparency compliance** → [mod-106 — Compensation Architecture & Total Rewards](../mod-106-compensation-architecture-and-total-rewards/).
- **Performance-review instrument, PIPs, RIFs, and IC separation agreements** → [mod-107 — Performance, Promotion & Offboarding](../mod-107-performance-promotion-and-offboarding/). mod-105 keeps *exec-separation substance*; mod-107 owns IC separation architecture.
- **Pay-equity programs, DEI-weighted comp decisions, and employee-wide comp communications** → [mod-108 — Culture, Employee Experience & DEI](../mod-108-culture-employee-experience-and-dei/).
- **Comp-data privacy, HRIS access control, and board-portal retention of comp-committee materials** → [mod-110 — Privacy, Data Governance & Sector Compliance](../mod-110-privacy-data-governance-and-sector-compliance/).
- **The comp committee as a board committee** (board-committee architecture, officer-appointment resolutions, D&O / indemnification agreements) → [mod-111 — Corporate Governance, Board Operations & Officer Duties](../mod-111-corporate-governance-board-operations-and-officer-duties/). mod-105 owns the *comp-committee charter and operating cadence*; mod-111 owns the *board-committee architecture* within which it sits.
- **EPLI coverage for exec-separation disputes and D&O coverage through the CoC window** → [mod-112 — Enterprise Risk, Insurance & Compliance](../mod-112-enterprise-risk-insurance-and-compliance/).
- **Per-country equity sub-plans** (UK EMI, Canadian stock-option variations, EU per-country sub-plans, Israeli § 102 trustee plans), EOR-vs-local-entity equity-grant interaction, and per-country statutory severance minimums → [mod-113 — International Expansion & Global Workforce](../mod-113-international-expansion-and-global-workforce/).
- **IC-level equity-administration mechanics** (Carta administrator operations, grant-letter mail-merge, 83(b) filing walk-through, cap-table reconciliation to the equity plan) → a future `startup-people-ops-controllership` track.

See [chapter 10](./10-ownership-boundary-map.md) for the full boundary map and the decision rule that routes an incoming equity-comp question to the right owner.

## What "done" looks like

By the end of this module you can, for a hypothetical corporation operating across seed through pre-IPO, produce and defend:

1. A grant-guideline table — function × level × stage × band, with the representation unit chosen for the stage, the benchmarking data sources named and dated, and the dilution budget reconciled against the current option pool.
2. An IC equity-plan structure memo — vehicle selection (ISO vs. NSO vs. PCRSU) with the § 422(d) tracking posture, vesting schedule, early-exercise and § 83(b) policy, post-termination exercise window, and IC-level acceleration treatment.
3. A refresh / promotion / retention grant policy — the annual top-up vs. four-year refresh decision, the promotion-driven refresh sizing table, the retention-grant exception rubric, and the written policy document that governs all three.
4. An executive compensation package for a specific role (CEO / CFO / CRO / CPO / CTO / GC) — base, bonus, equity, PSU overlay, severance, CoC acceleration, indemnification — with the negotiation posture and the comp-committee approval memo.
5. A compensation-committee charter and an independence-progression plan — composition, cadence, authority, advisor engagement, and the sequencing from Series-A "informal" to three-independent-director pre-IPO reconstitution.
6. An annual comp-cycle operating plan — timing, budget derivation, merit / equity / promotion split, manager → HRBP → executive → committee workflow, comp-statement templates, and the retrospective instrument.
7. A change-of-control equity policy — the single-vs-double-trigger landscape, the role-based acceleration ladder, the modified-single-trigger carveout for the CEO, and the § 280G interaction analysis.
8. An executive severance and release architecture — the exec severance ladder, release-of-claims mechanic, OWBPA / ADEA 21-day / 45-day / 7-day structure, and the day-one drafting posture that makes the day-of-departure execution routine.
9. A pre-IPO Reg S-K Item 402 disclosure readiness plan — the Summary Compensation Table build, the Grants of Plan-Based Awards and Outstanding Equity Awards tables, the CD&A drafting timeline, and the pay-vs.-performance posture under Item 402(v).
10. A defensible ownership-boundary map for every equity-comp question that lands — "does that belong here, or in the fundraising-finance track, the exit track, mod-103, mod-104, mod-106, mod-107, mod-108, mod-110, mod-111, mod-112, mod-113, or people-ops controllership?"
