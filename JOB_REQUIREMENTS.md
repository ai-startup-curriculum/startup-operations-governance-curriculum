## Status — 2026-10 live cycle, continuity wins again

Startup Operations & Governance covers the **People / Legal / Operations / Governance** pillar of the startup dependency graph — the operating infrastructure and governance apparatus that turns a founding-team hack into a scalable, compliant, and governable company. It runs from IDEA-stage entity formation through the pre-IPO COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary seat.

The 2026-10 live cycle sampled **45 in-window postings** across the equivalent-title buckets — 30 in the People / COO / Chief of Staff / BizOps / Total Rewards bucket and 15 in the Legal / Governance / Compliance / Privacy bucket. The cycle deliberately widened the two buckets flagged as undersampled in the 2026-09 next-cycle note: **Corporate Secretary moved from 1 in-window posting to 3** (Galaxy Digital, Culture Amp Global GC & Company Secretary, Omidyar Network GC-as-Corp-Secretary); **Data Protection Officer / Head of Privacy / Privacy Counsel moved from 0 direct in-window postings to 3** (Anthropic Privacy Counsel, Polymarket Director GRC & Privacy, Culture Amp owning global privacy strategy). All 14 curriculum-owned themes continue to register posting-level support. Theme-11 (Corporate Governance) rose from 0.32 to 0.33 and theme-12 (Enterprise Risk / Compliance) rose from 0.24 to 0.31 on the back of the better-balanced sample — both now at or above the 0.30 threshold. Theme-14 (Operations Function Design) held at 0.53 (vs 0.56). Theme-10 (Privacy / Data Governance) rose from 0.24 to 0.29 as the Privacy / DPO bucket widened. No emerging theme cleared the { ≥ 3 in-window postings, ≥ 0.30 frequency, no existing module can extend } threshold that would justify a new module. **The delta this cycle is therefore empty by design** — the four `emerging_themes_below_threshold` captured in the machine-readable JSON (AI-usage-policy authoring, fractional-executive delivery model, pay-transparency comp-band publication, RTO stance as a first-class posting signal) are all already covered inside existing modules; the pay-transparency and RTO signals have doubled or tripled in rate (now 0.56 and 0.71) which argues for reclassification to "baseline posting hygiene" rather than for a new module, since the practice is already pedagogised in mod-106 obj 5 and mod-108 obj 5.

Zero truly-new requirement patterns met the 3-posting threshold this cycle. Specifically ruled out: EU AI Act Article 55 GPAI named compliance (0 postings used Article 55 / GPAI language directly), SEC Climate Disclosure Rules (0 mentions), NY DFS Part 500 (0 direct mentions), EU DORA Digital Operational Resilience Act (0 mentions), UK FCA / HKMA (0 mentions in sample), and Trust & Safety governance distinct from traditional risk / compliance (adjacent engineering-side T&S postings exist but did not reach legal-role-ownership scope at 3+ postings).

## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary task domains at the *pillar* level from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json) `authoritative_references` — the DGCL / MBCA / Delaware case-law reference set, NVCA model documents, practitioner startup-legal libraries (Cooley GO, Orrick, WSGR), the federal employment-law reference set (FLSA, IRS, EEOC, USCIS, OSHA, WARN, NLRB), the equity-comp practitioner canon (Holloway, Carta), the compensation-benchmark set (Carta, Pave, Radford, Figures / Pequity / Assemble), the people / management / culture canon (McCord, Hughes Johnson, Gil, Hoffman & Yeh, Horowitz, Lencioni, Skelton & Pais, First Round Review), the privacy / data-governance framework (GDPR, CCPA / CPRA, IAPP tracker, HIPAA, SOC 2, NIST, IAB TCF), the corporate-governance / board-mechanics reference set (Society for Corporate Governance, CII, NYSE / Nasdaq, SEC Corp Fin, Woodruff Sawyer), the insurance / risk / anti-corruption / sanctions frameworks (Embroker / Vouch / Newfront, OFAC, BIS EAR, FCPA), the international-expansion / EOR / immigration frameworks (Remote / Deel / Oyster / Rippling EOR / Papaya, USCIS, OECD TP), the IP frameworks (USPTO, US Copyright, DTSA, OSI), and the operations / scaling canon (Scaling Up, Greiner, Adizes, OKRs, High Output Management).
2. Anchored the taxonomy against the three-axis model authored in [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) — the [dependency graph](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA.md) (People / Legal / Operations / Governance branch owned by this repo), the [stage ladder](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md) (IDEA → PRE-SEED → SEED → SERIES-A → GROWTH → MATURE), and the founder's operating loop (decide → allocate → communicate → measure) — none of which this track re-teaches.
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — this repo owns the People / Legal / Operations / Governance pillar depth from IDEA-stage entity formation through the pre-IPO COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary seat. Everything else defers per the *Ownership map — quick reference* section below.
4. This cycle: fanned parallel WebSearch across role-title landing pages on builtin.com / builtinnyc.com / builtinsf.com / builtinboston.com / builtinchicago.org / builtinaustin.com, jobs.ashbyhq.com, boards.greenhouse.io, Society for Corporate Governance careers board (careers.societycorpgov.org), and jobs.generalcatalyst.com / jobs.madrona.com (VC portfolio boards). Complemented with WebFetch of individual posting URLs where the target board returned HTML. Ashby pages are client-rendered and WebFetch returned empty bodies, so date_observed and salary bands were cross-verified via mirror sites (zapply, builtin, dreamworkhq, teal, freehire, startup.jobs); where a posting's date was ambiguous within July-October 2026 it was retained only when at least two mirror sources concurred on in-window status. startup.jobs role landing pages returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch and were captured via WebSearch summary. LinkedIn / Wellfound / YC Work-at-a-Startup / VC portfolio-jobs boards required authentication and were sampled indirectly via WebSearch summaries. Computed per-theme frequency against a strict n=45 in-window denominator.
5. Compared 2026-10 posting frequencies against the { ≥ 3 distinct in-window postings, ≥ 0.30 frequency, no existing module can extend } continuity-bias threshold. Every net-new theme candidate failed at least one gate — see *Emerging themes — below curriculum threshold this cycle* for the four candidates evaluated. Also re-tested emerging regulatory frameworks that could plausibly surface as new themes (EU AI Act Article 55 GPAI, SEC Climate Disclosure, NY DFS Part 500, EU DORA, UK FCA / HKMA, Trust & Safety governance) and none met the 3-posting threshold either.

## Ownership map — quick reference for the next cycle

When backfilling postings, use this ownership decision to keep this pillar from drifting into Foundations, Finance, GTM, or Exit territory:

- **Startup Operations & Governance** (this repo, level 50) — owns Legal Entity Formation & Corporate Structure, Founding-Team Legal Architecture, Employment Law & Worker Classification, Hiring & Onboarding Operations, Equity-Compensation *Policy* & the Compensation Committee, Compensation Architecture / Leveling / Total Rewards, Performance / Promotion / Development / Offboarding, Culture / Employee Experience / DEI / Handbook, Commercial Contracts & IP & Legal Ops, Privacy / Data Governance / Sector Compliance, Corporate Governance & Board Operations & Officer-Director Duties, Enterprise Risk & Insurance & Regulatory Compliance, International Expansion & Global Workforce & Immigration, and Operations-Function Design. IDEA-stage entity formation through the pre-IPO COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary seat.
- **Startup Foundations** (`startup-foundations`, level 10) — the *system*, the *graph*, the *stages*, the founder's operating loop, and the founder-numbers slice. Prerequisite. *Never* re-taught here.
- **Startup Finance & Fundraising** (`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`, level 40) — owns Startup Accounting, Unit Economics, Three-Statement Model, Cap Tables, Convertibles, Valuation, Fundraising Strategy, Term Sheets, Runway & Bridge Financing, Board & Investor Governance for the CFO, Finance Operations. Sideways — that pillar owns equity *economics* (dilution / waterfall / 409A); this repo owns equity *policy*. That pillar owns the CFO-side of board and investor governance; this repo owns the governance operating machinery.
- **Startup Product & GTM** (`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`, level 30) — owns the customer-facing branch (Discovery, PMF, Positioning, Founder-Led Sales, Pricing, Motion, Demand Gen, Retention, GTM Metrics & First Hires). Sideways — that pillar owns GTM-role-specific hiring / ramping / sales-comp; this repo owns the general hiring / total-rewards infrastructure.
- **Startup Exit & Endgame** (`startup-exit-curriculum`, level 40) — M&A, secondaries, IPO, succession, shutdown *transactions*. Sideways — this repo produces the ongoing-company governance infrastructure and pre-transaction readiness; that track owns transaction-execution mechanics.
- **Founder / CEO** (`founder-ceo-curriculum`, level 30) — sideways role-pathway peer — references this curriculum at ~60–70% coverage for the CEO seat.
- **CPO (product)** and **CTO** (`cpo-curriculum`, `cto-curriculum`, level 30) — role pathways that reference this curriculum for People / hiring / performance / leveling / IP / employment-contract depth, and add function-specific curated views.

## Next cycle — targeted expansion

The 2026-10 cycle closed the two undersampled cells flagged in the 2026-09 cycle: Corporate Secretary rose from 1 to 3 in-window postings (Galaxy Digital, Culture Amp, Omidyar Network), and DPO / Head of Privacy / Privacy Counsel rose from 0 to 3 (Anthropic, Polymarket, Culture Amp). Both can continue to be targeted deliberately next cycle to maintain the 3-in-window floor.

Priorities for 2026-11:

1. **Reclassify "emerging-signal" into "baseline" where appropriate.** Pay-transparency comp-band publication (0.56) and RTO-stance labelling (0.71) have both doubled or tripled since 2026-09 and now look like baseline posting hygiene. Next cycle should promote these out of the emerging-themes table into the "standard practice" treatment inside mod-106 obj 5 and mod-108 obj 5 respectively, and should authorise the mod-106 exercise-05 extension to add EU Pay Transparency Directive (transposition deadline 2026-06-07) posting-level enforcement patterns.
2. **Watch for the EU AI Act Article 55 GPAI compliance surface to clear 0.30.** The current 0.13 explicit rate was flat vs the prior cycle. Enforcement widens through Q1 2027 and may push explicit GPAI-named language above 0.30 in the next 60-90 days. Watch Culture Amp / Anthropic / Omidyar / Zapier-tier GC postings and the UK/EU DPO corpus.
3. **Target EU DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) and SEC Climate Disclosure Rules.** Both are plausible future entrants in fintech and growth-stage postings but surfaced zero mentions in this cycle. Monitor Range, Zero Hash, Crypto.com CDNA, Toku, and other fintech / crypto GC postings for the DORA / NY DFS Part 500 surface.
4. **Widen the Workforce + Immigration bucket.** Theme-13 (international expansion + immigration) sat at 0.18 this cycle, down from 0.36 — the drop reflects fewer explicitly-global People-org postings in the US sample. Next cycle should include more EOR / global-mobility specialists and immigration-counsel-adjacent postings to re-calibrate.

Continue to filter out narrow-scope IC recruiter / IC-employment-lawyer / IC product-counsel / IC privacy-counsel / payroll-specialist / office-manager postings whose scope is limited to one operational sub-domain — those defer down to a future startup-people-ops-controllership or startup-legal-ops-controllership track.
