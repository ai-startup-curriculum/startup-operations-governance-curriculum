## Status — bootstrap session, live postings deferred

Startup Operations & Governance covers the **People / Legal / Operations / Governance** pillar of the startup dependency graph — the operating infrastructure and governance apparatus that turns a founding-team hack into a scalable, compliant, and governable company. It runs from IDEA-stage entity formation through the pre-IPO COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary seat. Unlike a single-title role, the equivalent live-market postings span several hireable titles across four sub-pillars and multiple company stages. The live cycle should backfill from:

- **COO adjacency:** *Chief Operating Officer*, *Founding COO*, *Fractional / Interim COO* at Series-A → pre-IPO startups. Boards: LinkedIn, executive-search firms (Heidrick, Spencer Stuart, True Search, Daversa, Bolster, Riviera Partners), and VC portfolio-jobs pages (a16z, Sequoia, Bessemer, Accel, GV, Founders Fund, USV, Redpoint, Kleiner Perkins, Greylock, Lightspeed).
- **Chief-of-Staff adjacency:** *Chief of Staff to the CEO* / *Business Operations Lead* / *Head of Business Operations* at seed → Series-C startups.
- **People-org titles:** *Head of People*, *VP People*, *Chief People Officer (CPO — people, not product)*, *People Lead (founding)*, *Head of Talent*, *Head of People Operations*, *Head of Total Rewards*, *Head of Talent Acquisition* at seed → growth-stage companies.
- **Legal-org titles:** *General Counsel*, *VP Legal*, *Head of Legal*, *Deputy General Counsel*, *Fractional / Interim General Counsel* at Series-A → pre-IPO companies. Reference the TechGC community's fractional-GC-registry pattern for fractional titles.
- **Governance / compliance / corporate-secretary titles:** *Corporate Secretary*, *Assistant Corporate Secretary*, *Head of Corporate Governance*, *Governance & Compliance Lead*, *Chief Compliance Officer* at Series-B → pre-IPO companies.
- **Privacy / DPO titles:** *Head of Privacy*, *Chief Privacy Officer*, *Data Protection Officer (DPO)*, *Privacy Counsel* at Series-B → pre-IPO companies in regulated sectors (healthtech, fintech, edtech, ad-tech, enterprise SaaS).
- **Operations specialist titles:** *Head of Workplace*, *Head of Real Estate & Facilities*, *Head of Procurement*, *Head of Business Systems* at Series-B → growth-stage companies.

Postings for **pure recruiter / sourcer / IC-employment-lawyer / payroll-specialist / office-manager roles whose scope is limited to one operational sub-domain** should defer *down* to a future `startup-people-ops-controllership` or `startup-legal-ops-controllership` track — those cover the mechanical execution layer but not the pillar-level ownership this track owns.

## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary task domains at the *pillar* level from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json) `authoritative_references` — the DGCL / MBCA / Delaware case-law reference set, NVCA model documents, practitioner startup-legal libraries (Cooley GO, Orrick, WSGR), the federal employment-law reference set (FLSA, IRS, EEOC, USCIS, OSHA, WARN, NLRB), the equity-comp practitioner canon (Holloway, Carta), the compensation-benchmark set (Carta, Pave, Radford, Figures / Pequity / Assemble), the people / management / culture canon (McCord, Hughes Johnson, Gil, Hoffman & Yeh, Horowitz, Lencioni, Skelton & Pais, First Round Review), the privacy / data-governance framework (GDPR, CCPA / CPRA, IAPP tracker, HIPAA, SOC 2, NIST, IAB TCF), the corporate-governance / board-mechanics reference set (Society for Corporate Governance, CII, NYSE / Nasdaq, SEC Corp Fin, Woodruff Sawyer), the insurance / risk / anti-corruption / sanctions frameworks (Embroker / Vouch / Newfront, OFAC, BIS EAR, FCPA), the international-expansion / EOR / immigration frameworks (Remote / Deel / Oyster / Rippling EOR / Papaya, USCIS, OECD TP), the IP frameworks (USPTO, US Copyright, DTSA, OSI), and the operations / scaling canon (Scaling Up, Greiner, Adizes, OKRs, High Output Management).
2. Anchored the taxonomy against the three-axis model authored in [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) — the [dependency graph](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA.md) (People / Legal / Operations / Governance branch owned by this repo), the [stage ladder](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md) (IDEA → PRE-SEED → SEED → SERIES-A → GROWTH → MATURE), and the founder's operating loop (decide → allocate → communicate → measure) — none of which this track re-teaches.
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — this repo owns the People / Legal / Operations / Governance pillar depth from IDEA-stage entity formation through the pre-IPO COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary seat. Everything else defers:
   - *Down* to [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) (level 10) for the startup-as-a-system model, dependency graph, stage axis, founder's operating loop, and founder-numbers slice. Never re-taught.
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum) (level 40) for equity *economics* (dilution math, option-pool math, waterfall, 409A, tax-preference mechanics) — this track owns equity-comp *policy* — and for the CFO-side of board and investor governance (monthly updates, quarterly board packs, KPI dashboards reconciled to the financial model) — this track owns the governance operating machinery that wraps the CFO's artifacts.
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum) (level 30) for GTM-role-specific hiring / ramping (SDR / AE / CSM leveling, quota / capacity models, ramping playbooks, sales-comp plan design as a GTM instrument) — this track owns the general hiring / onboarding / performance / total-rewards infrastructure into which GTM-role-specific playbooks slot.
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-exit-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-exit-curriculum) (level 40) for M&A / IPO / secondary-transaction / shutdown *transaction* mechanics — this track owns the ongoing-company governance infrastructure, change-of-control equity policy (double-trigger acceleration, ESPP-in-transaction), employee-communications-during-a-transaction playbook, and the pre-transaction legal / HR / privacy / compliance readiness that feed those transactions.
   - *Up* to [`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum), [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum), and [`cto-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum) — role-pathway repos that reference this curriculum rather than re-authoring it, plus a future `coo-curriculum` / `chief-people-officer-curriculum` / `general-counsel-curriculum` layer.
   - *Cross-family* to [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) / [`ai-infra-security-solutions`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-security-solutions) for security-engineering depth (this track owns the SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / HIPAA *governance* boundary and hands off the engineering content), and to [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) / [`head-of-ai-governance-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) / [`ai-risk-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-risk-engineer-learning) for AI-safety-technical depth (this track owns the corporate-governance / employment-policy / commercial-contract machinery around AI usage and hands off the AI-safety content).
4. Sized each module against the level-50 depth-and-leadership target (~430–470h total across modules + projects), stage-tagged per [`STARTUP_STAGES.md`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md) — mod-101 → IDEA / PRE-SEED, mod-102 → PRE-SEED / SEED, mod-103–104 → SEED / SERIES-A, mod-105–109 → SEED / SERIES-A / SERIES-B (deepen at each), mod-110 → SERIES-A / B / C, mod-111 → SERIES-A / B / C, mod-112 → SERIES-B / C, mod-113 → SERIES-B / C, mod-114 → SERIES-A / B / C.
5. Flagged every theme's frequency as `needs-research` so the first live research cycle can populate the number without restructuring the catalog.

## Ownership map — quick reference for the next cycle

When backfilling postings, use this ownership decision to keep this pillar from drifting into Foundations, Finance, GTM, or Exit territory:

- **Startup Operations & Governance** (this repo, level 50) — owns Legal Entity Formation & Corporate Structure, Founding-Team Legal Architecture, Employment Law & Worker Classification, Hiring & Onboarding Operations, Equity-Compensation *Policy* & the Compensation Committee, Compensation Architecture / Leveling / Total Rewards, Performance / Promotion / Development / Offboarding, Culture / Employee Experience / DEI / Handbook, Commercial Contracts & IP & Legal Ops, Privacy / Data Governance / Sector Compliance, Corporate Governance & Board Operations & Officer-Director Duties, Enterprise Risk & Insurance & Regulatory Compliance, International Expansion & Global Workforce & Immigration, and Operations-Function Design. IDEA-stage entity formation through the pre-IPO COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary seat.
- **Startup Foundations** (`startup-foundations`, level 10) — the *system*, the *graph*, the *stages*, the founder's operating loop, and the founder-numbers slice. Prerequisite. *Never* re-taught here.
- **Startup Finance & Fundraising** (`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`, level 40) — owns Startup Accounting, Unit Economics, Three-Statement Model, Cap Tables, Convertibles, Valuation, Fundraising Strategy, Term Sheets, Runway & Bridge Financing, Board & Investor Governance for the CFO, Finance Operations. Sideways — this pillar owns equity *economics* (dilution / waterfall / 409A); this repo owns equity *policy*. This pillar owns the CFO-side of board and investor governance; this repo owns the governance operating machinery.
- **Startup Product & GTM** (`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`, level 30) — owns the customer-facing branch (Discovery, PMF, Positioning, Founder-Led Sales, Pricing, Motion, Demand Gen, Retention, GTM Metrics & First Hires). Sideways — this pillar owns GTM-role-specific hiring / ramping / sales-comp; this repo owns the general hiring / total-rewards infrastructure.
- **Startup Exit & Endgame** (`startup-exit-curriculum`, level 40) — M&A, secondaries, IPO, succession, shutdown *transactions*. Sideways — this repo produces the ongoing-company governance infrastructure and pre-transaction readiness; that track owns transaction-execution mechanics.
- **Founder / CEO** (`founder-ceo-curriculum`, level 30) — sideways role-pathway peer — references this curriculum at ~60–70% coverage for the CEO seat.
- **CPO (product)** and **CTO** (`cpo-curriculum`, `cto-curriculum`, level 30) — role pathways that reference this curriculum for People / hiring / performance / leveling / IP / employment-contract depth, and add function-specific curated views.

## Salary evidence

Not gathered this cycle — WebSearch / WebFetch gated. On the first live cycle, the salary summary should aggregate published US ranges **per equivalent-title bucket AND per company stage** (COO comp at growth-stage differs materially from Chief-of-Staff comp at seed, Head-of-People at Series-B differs from CPO at pre-IPO, and full GC comp at pre-IPO is not the Head-of-Legal comp at Series-A; a single aggregate would mislead):

- Founder / CEO (pre-seed → seed, no COO / CPO / GC yet) — cash + founder equity
- Fractional / Interim COO or GC (pre-Series-A) — monthly retainer + equity
- Founding COO / Founding Chief of Staff / Founding Head of People (seed → Series-A, <30 headcount)
- Chief of Staff to CEO (seed → Series-C) — grows into a COO seat or a BizOps org
- Head of People / VP People / CPO (people, not product) (Series-A → growth)
- Head of Total Rewards / Head of Talent Acquisition (Series-B → growth)
- General Counsel / VP Legal / Head of Legal (Series-A → pre-IPO)
- Corporate Secretary / Head of Corporate Governance (Series-B → pre-IPO)
- Chief Privacy Officer / Head of Privacy / Data Protection Officer (Series-B → pre-IPO)
- COO (Series-B → pre-IPO)

Do not quote a headline range until the strict per-bucket-and-stage sample clears **n≥8 with cash + equity comp present**. Executive-level comp packages are almost always cash + equity + performance bonus; recording only cash would materially understate the total. Fractional-COO / Fractional-GC comp is usually a monthly retainer plus equity — the live cycle must record which structure was quoted and normalise before aggregating. State-level pay-transparency-law-derived ranges (Colorado, California, New York City, Washington, Illinois) are the highest-signal source for backfill because they are legally-required, structured, and comparable across postings; national ranges without a jurisdiction anchor should be triangulated against the state-transparency-derived ranges before publication. Use [BLS OEWS](https://www.bls.gov/oes/current/) for HR Managers / General & Operations Managers / Chief Executives / Lawyers / Compliance Officers as the public-benchmark anchor and [Carta Total Comp](https://carta.com/data/) / [Pave](https://www.pave.com/benchmarks) / [Radford](https://radford.aon.com/) as the private-source anchors.
