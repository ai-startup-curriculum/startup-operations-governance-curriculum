## Status — 2026-09 live cycle, continuity wins

Startup Operations & Governance covers the **People / Legal / Operations / Governance** pillar of the startup dependency graph — the operating infrastructure and governance apparatus that turns a founding-team hack into a scalable, compliant, and governable company. It runs from IDEA-stage entity formation through the pre-IPO COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary seat.

The live cycle sampled 30 postings across the equivalent-title buckets called out in the previous scaffold (COO, Founding COO, Chief of Staff, Head of People / VP People / CPO-people, Head of People Operations, Head of Total Rewards, General Counsel / Head of Legal / Fractional GC, Corporate Secretary, Chief Compliance Officer / Fractional CCO, Head of Business Operations); 25 were in-window; 5 older exec-search / Ashby postings were retained for title-bucket coverage but excluded from the per-theme denominator. **All 14 curriculum-owned themes registered ≥ 0.24 frequency and 10 of 14 cleared the ≥ 0.30 continuity-bias threshold** — the sample confirms the existing catalog matches the 2026 market. No emerging theme cleared the { ≥ 3 in-window postings, ≥ 0.30 frequency, no existing module can extend } threshold that would justify a new module. **The delta this cycle is therefore empty by design** — the four `emerging_themes_below_threshold` captured in the machine-readable JSON (AI-usage-policy authoring, fractional-executive delivery model, pay-transparency-driven comp-band publication, RTO stance as a first-class posting signal) are all already covered inside existing modules and only warrant incremental exercise-level extensions on future cycles.

2. 3. Applied the **ownership rule** — this repo owns the People / Legal / Operations / Governance pillar depth from IDEA-stage entity formation through the pre-IPO COO / CPO / GC / Corporate-Secretary seat. Everything else defers per the *Ownership map — quick reference* section below.
4. This cycle: fanned WebSearch across role-title landing pages on startup.jobs, builtin.com / builtinnyc.com / builtinsf.com / builtinboston.com / builtinchicago.org, jobs.ashbyhq.com, and boards.greenhouse.io; complemented with WebFetch of individual posting URLs where the target board returned HTML (startup.jobs role landing pages returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch and were captured via WebSearch summary; LinkedIn / Wellfound / YC Work-at-a-Startup / VC portfolio-jobs boards required authentication and were sampled indirectly via WebSearch summaries). Computed per-theme frequency against a strict n=25 in-window denominator; 5 near-window / older postings retained for title-bucket coverage were excluded from the denominator.
5. Compared 2026-09 posting frequencies against the { ≥ 3 distinct in-window postings, ≥ 0.30 frequency, no existing module can extend } continuity-bias threshold. Every net-new theme candidate failed at least one gate — see *Emerging themes — below curriculum threshold this cycle* for the four candidates evaluated.

## Ownership map — quick reference for the next cycle

When backfilling postings, use this ownership decision to keep this pillar from drifting into Foundations, Finance, GTM, or Exit territory:

- **Startup Operations & Governance** (this repo, level 50) — owns Legal Entity Formation & Corporate Structure, Founding-Team Legal Architecture, Employment Law & Worker Classification, Hiring & Onboarding Operations, Equity-Compensation *Policy* & the Compensation Committee, Compensation Architecture / Leveling / Total Rewards, Performance / Promotion / Development / Offboarding, Culture / Employee Experience / DEI / Handbook, Commercial Contracts & IP & Legal Ops, Privacy / Data Governance / Sector Compliance, Corporate Governance & Board Operations & Officer-Director Duties, Enterprise Risk & Insurance & Regulatory Compliance, International Expansion & Global Workforce & Immigration, and Operations-Function Design. For context, aggregate public anchors from 2026:

- **VP People (Remote US, Built In 2026 salary data):** avg base $206,192, avg additional cash $39,862, avg total cash comp $246,054; most common base range $170K–$180K.
- **Startup General Counsel (US, ZipRecruiter 2026):** avg $151,643, common range $108,500–$190,000 (aggregate skews low — mixes fractional / interim / small-firm postings; the Outset AI Series B in-house GC band runs materially above this).

- **Fintech Chief Compliance Officer (US, ZipRecruiter 2026):** avg $124,409, common range $93,000–$146,000.

Executive comp packages (COO, CPO, GC, CCO) are almost always cash + equity + performance bonus; the ranges above capture cash only where equity is present but undisclosed in magnitude. Fractional-COO / Fractional-GC / Fractional-CCO comp (Wheel, SylloTips) is typically a monthly retainer + equity — normalise before aggregating. State-level pay-transparency-law-derived ranges are the highest-signal source; national aggregates without a jurisdiction anchor skew low because they mix fractional / interim / small-firm postings into the aggregate.
